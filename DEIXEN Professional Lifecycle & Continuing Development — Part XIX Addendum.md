# PART XIX — FROM BEGINNER LEARNING TO CONTINUING PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

## 94. DEIXEN's intended relationship with the learner should not end at the first job

DEIXEN should not be designed only around a short-lived transaction in which a beginner purchases a course, completes it, and leaves as soon as they obtain a job.

The longer-term ambition is to support a person's professional development across more than one stage:

1. **Explore:** understand DEIXEN's approach and try a small, genuine operational-learning experience before paying.
2. **Learn and practise:** progress through a structured path from beginner knowledge to supported practice, integrated work tasks, independent assessment, and evidence-backed development.
3. **Enter professional work:** use the learning and evidence as preparation for applying to roles and transitioning into a real workplace, without treating DEIXEN completion as a job guarantee or employer authorization.
4. **Continue as a working professional:** return to practise less-frequent or difficult cases, refresh knowledge, explore verified new workflows, and develop capabilities relevant to changing responsibilities.
5. **Continue professional development:** build broader capability over time as roles, markets, tools, systems, and professional responsibilities evolve.

The product should create a credible reason for a learner to remain a DEIXEN customer after employment because the platform continues to provide value—not because the learner is made dependent on it or prevented from leaving.

## 95. Keep the professional-development lifecycle distinct from the three existing lifecycles

This continuing relationship is an additional strategic/business lens. It does not collapse or replace the three lifecycles already described in Part XI and Part XVI:

- **Customer Journey:** the needs, choices, service, fulfilment, changes, and follow-up experienced by the travel customer.
- **Professional/Learner Journey:** the individual's development from learning through supported practice, independent performance, and further development.
- **Work Task Lifecycle:** the steps and outcome of a specific piece of professional work.

The proposed **Learner-to-Professional Development Lifecycle** describes how DEIXEN remains useful across a person's career stages. It can connect to the professional/learner journey, but it is not the same as one lesson sequence, one work task, or one customer transaction.

Employment is a possible transition and an important intended outcome—not a guaranteed result, a competence shortcut, or a reason to claim that a learner is workplace-ready. A learner may already be employed while still using DEIXEN to develop, or may complete a learning path without immediately finding a role.

Do not require a learner to disclose employment status to access their learning record. Any employment information used for personalization or research should be voluntary, proportionate, and handled under an approved privacy policy.

## 96. Continuing value for a working professional

The post-foundation offer should be grounded in substantive professional value. Candidate areas include:

- **Skill maintenance and refresh:** revisit important procedures and practise capabilities that are not used frequently enough to remain fluent.
- **Uncommon and difficult cases:** practise situations that may be costly, stressful, or rare in day-to-day work, using verified system behavior and appropriate professional guidance.
- **Advanced work domains:** progressively explore changes, cancellations, refunds, exchanges, ticketing, queues, disruptions, after-sales work, and other role-relevant areas only as their operational scope and content are researched and verified.
- **System and procedure updates:** learn about changes only when they are supported by current, authoritative references; distinguish Amadeus behavior from airline, employer, office, market, or regulatory rules.
- **Professional judgment and customer service:** develop needs discovery, transparent advice, expectation management, service recovery, communication, and appropriate escalation through reviewed cases.
- **Retention and transfer practice:** return to structurally varied tasks and delayed probes to understand what remains reliable and what needs reinforcement.
- **Role-specific development:** select learning and cases relevant to the learner's current or intended responsibilities, without implying that one generic path fits every employer.
- **Evidence portfolio continuity:** retain an honest record of what the learner has demonstrated, under what conditions, and what remains untested. A historical record must not be converted into an unsupported employment credential.

These are candidate value streams, not a promise that all such content is currently present or that every item must be built. Prioritize them through market/practitioner evidence, verified references, learner demand, and sustainable content operations.

## 97. Retention must come from earned value, not artificial dependence

DEIXEN should aim for a durable professional relationship, but customer retention must be ethical.

- Do not hide cancellation, create artificial urgency, punish a learner for leaving, or use streaks/points/leaderboards to make the learner feel unable to stop.
- Do not remove or falsify previously earned learning evidence merely because a paid access period expires. Preserve the learner's legitimate historical record under the applicable data-retention and privacy rules; distinguish read-only record access from paid practice/content access.
- Do not imply that the learner cannot perform a job without an active DEIXEN subscription.
- Do not make an employee's access to an employer's real systems, bookings, or work authorization depend on a consumer subscription.
- Offer clear access periods, renewal terms, cancellation controls, payment receipts, and applicable refund/tax information before purchase.
- Give the learner a natural point to pause or leave after achieving their current goal. Returning later should be straightforward and should not require buying the same completed foundation again unless the learner explicitly chooses a refresher.
- Continuing membership should contain genuinely new or recurring value; merely leaving old lessons unlocked is not enough to justify indefinite billing.

The target is voluntary continuation because DEIXEN remains useful, not lock-in.

## 98. Measure learning success separately from continuing commercial value

DEIXEN must not treat subscription duration, time in the product, renewal, or payment as evidence of professional competence.

Track two distinct outcome families:

**A. Learning and professional-development outcomes**

- completion of intended learning activities, interpreted cautiously;
- independently demonstrated performance under defined task conditions;
- error detection, recovery, and appropriate escalation;
- retention after a meaningful interval;
- transfer to structurally different cases;
- limitations and untested areas of the evidence;
- where feasible, practitioner/employer feedback from real work contexts.

**B. Customer and business outcomes**

- Explore-to-paid conversion;
- price acceptance by market and learner segment;
- paid-path purchase and completion patterns;
- renewal and continuation after the initial learning period;
- reasons for cancellation, pause, or non-renewal;
- use of continuing-development content by working professionals;
- support burden, content maintenance cost, payment fees, refunds, and contribution margin.

These measures answer different questions. A learner who reaches their goal and does not renew may represent a successful learning outcome and a valid customer decision. A customer who renews frequently may still not be learning effectively. Do not optimize retention at the expense of learner success, evidence integrity, or honest communication.

## 99. Connect continuing development to the five existing product areas

The continuing-professional concept should initially be explored within the existing five areas, not by adding another top-level area:

- **Home:** present a relevant next action based on the learner's chosen goal and evidence, such as a refresher, new case, or advanced learning path.
- **Learning:** provide foundation, advanced, refresher, and role-relevant learning when content is validated.
- **Terminal:** remain the operational simulation environment for practice, scenarios, and protected assessment. It is not a live production reservation system unless a separately authorized real environment is explicitly established.
- **Scenario Bank:** organize routine, uncommon, complex, changed, and service-recovery work cases with clear purpose, prerequisites, verification status, and eligibility.
- **Growth:** preserve an honest evidence portfolio and explain where additional practice or new evidence may be useful. It must not become a numeric employability meter or an artificial reason to subscribe.

The current five-area structure, D92, D97, the Terminal transcript rules, the distinction between Customer and Coach, and the Amadeus Verified Reference authority remain unchanged by this proposal.

## 100. Questions and validation gates before commercial adoption

Before building a continuing-development offer or claiming that DEIXEN remains valuable after employment, investigate:

1. Which recurring professional problems do working reservation/ticketing employees want to practise outside their employer's systems?
2. Which cases are genuinely rare or difficult, and which are simply advanced curriculum content?
3. How often do procedures, tools, market requirements, or role responsibilities change, and which updates can DEIXEN reliably verify and maintain?
4. What do working professionals in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Oman, and the UAE consider worth paying for after they have learned the foundations?
5. Would a working professional prefer monthly access, a 3-month pack, a 6-month pack, or pay-per-program access—and why?
6. What evidence portfolio or refresher functions are genuinely useful without becoming a substitute for employer supervision, real workplace practice, or an official qualification?
7. What proportion of continuing revenue can sustainably fund verified content maintenance, support, platform operations, and practitioner review?
8. Does the continuing offer improve professional development outcomes, or does it merely increase time spent in the product?

The smallest validation should include interviews with working practitioners, a limited continuing-development prototype, transparent price tests, and measurement of actual purchase/renewal behavior. Do not build a large post-employment feature suite before identifying a recurring problem that users recognize and will pay to solve.
