Scenario: the passenger refuses to give a mobile number

Mr Fahad Alqahtani wants one seat from Jeddah (JED) to Dubai (DXB) on 25OCT, on flight 6X 515 in class B. Your agency's phone: 966110000000. When you ask for his mobile number or e-mail for the airline, he says he does not want to share them. Ticketing arrangement: TKOK. He requested the booking himself. Build the booking, end the transaction, then price it.

Objective

Complete and price the booking while recording, correctly, that the passenger refused to share contact details with the airline.

Constraints

One adult, one flight, class B on 6X 515. No mobile number or e-mail is available. Do not invent contact details and do not bypass the end-of-transaction warning.
