## Proposed insertion

### Application of DEIXEN Specialist Standards

For any material task, identify the relevant specialist standards in `/standards/README.md` and read the owning standard itself. Do not rely on a remembered summary or on this index when the exact rule matters.

Before applying a standard, check its current status, version, scope, and authority. Apply approved standards within their stated scope. Treat proposed, draft, research-required, practitioner-validation-required, or empirically unvalidated material as a proposal or evidence gap—not as an adopted project rule.

Keep the following separate:

1. **Project authority:** current canonical decisions and the relevant owning architecture/specification.
2. **Domain truth:** the source specifically authorized for that domain.
3. **Quality standard:** how the relevant class of work is judged.
4. **Task acceptance criteria:** what this particular assignment must deliver.
5. **Validation evidence:** what was actually inspected, tested, researched, or observed.

Do not copy a specialist standard into this file or another unrelated document. The AI Working Rules govern how the agent works; the standards library and domain-owning documents govern their respective quality requirements.

If a task has no approved applicable standard, state that clearly. You may propose temporary, task-specific acceptance criteria, but label any new lasting requirement as a proposal and obtain the required owner decision before treating it as binding.

If a specialist standard appears to conflict with a canonical decision or the owning domain authority, do not silently reconcile the conflict. Preserve both statements, identify the conflict and its consequence, and route it through the existing authority and change-control process.

For Amadeus Cryptic content, D12/D15 and the current `DEIXEN_Amadeus_Verified_Reference.md` remain controlling. Trace the exact claim to the applicable `V-ID`; preserve unresolved `U` items as unresolved; do not invent commands, outputs, error messages, prerequisites, or recovery behavior. A simulator implementation or AI-generated explanation is not a substitute for Amadeus verification.

For lessons and curriculum, respect D95 and D96 and the exact adopted v1.5 criteria within their stated scope. Do not reproduce, revise, or extend the 13-criterion framework from an incomplete summary or a different historical report. Recover and inspect the exact adopted document first. Any additional requirement concerning learner adaptation, support fading, or cross-lesson continuity must be reconciled with current curriculum/Coach/UX decisions and validated before being treated as a new binding rule.

For learning-science claims, identify the source quality, context, applicability, and limitations. General research may inform a design decision; it does not prove DEIXEN's own effectiveness. Claims about DEIXEN outcomes require appropriate DEIXEN-specific evaluation.

For customer interaction and professional realism, distinguish practitioner observations, industry guidance, research findings, and owner decisions. Do not treat a single practitioner's view as universal practice or as proof of Amadeus behavior. Keep customer dialogue, Coach instruction, operational output, and learner evidence distinct.

In every material report, state which standards and owning sources were actually consulted and identify important items that remain unresolved. Never claim that all applicable standards were checked when a source was unavailable or not inspected.

## Why this belongs in file 08

This proposed section adds only an agent-behavior control: **find the relevant standard, inspect its authority, apply it within scope, and report gaps honestly**. It deliberately does not reproduce Amadeus, lesson-writing, learning-science, customer-service, or design requirements. Those remain in their owning references.
