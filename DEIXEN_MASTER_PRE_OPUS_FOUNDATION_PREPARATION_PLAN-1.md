# DEIXEN — MASTER PRE-OPUS FOUNDATION PREPARATION PLAN

**Former Working Name:** AeroBridge  
**Current Working Name:** DEIXEN  
**Status:** WORKING PLAN — PRE-OPUS  
**Purpose:** Prepare the strongest and most accurate project Corpus possible for independent review by Opus.  
**Scope:** From waiting for the Amadeus package through final handoff of the complete prepared Corpus to Opus.  
**Out of Scope:** Resolving Opus findings, post-Opus closure, final canonicalization after Opus, design execution, implementation, QA, or any downstream stage that depends on the independent Opus review.

---

## 1. Mission

The objective of this plan is to prepare DEIXEN with the strongest Foundation that Sonnet, Karim, and ChatGPT can responsibly achieve before Opus enters the project.

This plan is not merely about:

- cleaning files
- improving wording
- reducing file count
- standardizing formatting
- or preparing a formal Handoff to Opus

The objective is to produce a Corpus that is:

- evidence-grounded
- internally coherent
- authority-aware
- synchronized
- traceable
- explicit about uncertainty
- technically and conceptually strengthened
- cross-artifact consistent
- ready for independent adversarial review

Every material result produced during the pre-Opus phase should be answerable in terms of:

1. What is the source of this information?
2. What is the confidence/evidence level?
3. Who has authority over this information?
4. Is it established truth, a decision, a recommendation, a hypothesis, or historical material?
5. Does it conflict with another artifact?
6. Which artifacts are affected by it?
7. Have affected artifacts been updated and rechecked?
8. Is external / SME validation still required?
9. Is this a point that should deliberately remain open for Opus to challenge?
10. Can Opus understand what was done and why without relying on conversational memory?

## 2. Governing Objective

The governing standard for this phase is:

> **Verify as much as possible. Strengthen as much as possible. Synchronize everything that is legitimately connected. Preserve uncertainty where verification is unavailable. Leave genuinely Opus-dependent judgment to Opus.**

In practice:

- establish what can be established
- correct what can be corrected
- improve what can be responsibly improved
- synchronize what must be synchronized
- do not invent what is not known
- do not pre-empt Opus where genuinely independent judgment is useful

## 3. Authority and Epistemic Discipline

This plan does not create a new authority hierarchy for the project.

The following categories must remain distinct:

- Evidence
- Domain Truth
- Implementation Truth
- Project Decision
- Recommendation
- Hypothesis
- Historical Record
- Planned Work
- Open Question
- External Validation Requirement

One category must not silently be converted into another merely because the wording becomes more confident, more detailed, or more polished.

### 3.1 No Self-Declared Truth

No artifact becomes Truth merely because:

- Sonnet wrote it
- ChatGPT reviewed it
- it has existed for a long time
- it is the newest artifact
- it appears more authoritative or professional
- it matches intuition
- or the word `Canonical` appears in its title

Claims must remain traceable through:

> **claim → evidence → authority → status → affected artifacts**

### 3.2 Existing Canonical Material Is Not Automatically Correct

An artifact being canonical in project status does not mean every statement inside it is permanently correct.

During this phase, the Corpus must be tested for:

- contradictions
- stale statements
- scope drift
- outdated references
- authority confusion
- undocumented assumptions
- broken dependencies
- claims whose evidence or applicability has expired

A closed decision must not be changed merely because a local review produces an alternative that appears preferable.

Any material change must follow the appropriate evidence, authority, and decision path.

The transition from **AeroBridge** to **DEIXEN** is a formal part of the Pre-Opus Foundation Preparation and must not be treated as a purely cosmetic rename.

The objective is for Opus to receive a Corpus with one coherent current identity, without accidental coexistence of AeroBridge and DEIXEN across current project materials.

#### 3.3.2 DEIXEN Naming Basis

DEIXEN was not selected randomly.

It emerged after applying a strict set of naming criteria, including must-have conditions and secondary preferences.

These criteria are retained so that any reviewer, including Opus, can understand the reasoning behind the current working name without reconstructing the naming history from conversational memory.

#### 3.3.3 Naming Criteria

The current working name was expected to satisfy the following criteria:

1. Be one cohesive name rather than an obvious construction of two joined words or fragments.
2. Be relatively short, preferably around 2–3 syllables, and easy to pronounce and remember.
3. Have a professional, premium, institutional, technical, and authoritative character rather than a generic SaaS/startup feel.
4. Feel modern without falling into AI, neural, futuristic, crypto, or generic-tech naming clichés.
5. Support a strong visual identity, with particular value placed on visually distinctive letters such as X, V, Z, C, K, and similar forms.
6. Have a real linguistic or conceptual basis rather than being a random arrangement of letters chosen only for technical appearance.
7. Have semantic relevance to:
   **demonstrate / make evident / show**

   This aligns with the product philosophy that competence is demonstrated through performance rather than merely claimed as knowledge.
8. Remain viable beyond Aviation and therefore not be literally tied to aviation, airports, or GDS terminology.
9. Avoid obvious commercial collision with companies, software, education, AI, aviation brands, or closely related trademarks based on the project's preliminary screening.
10. Pass an appropriate preliminary screening across, as applicable:
   - WIPO
   - Egypt
   - USA
   - EU
   - UK
   - company presence
   - web presence
11. Preserve the distinction between:
   - web/company presence
   - trademark registration
   - legal clearance
12. Be phonetically and visually distinctive, with:
   **sharp consonants + clean vowel structure + modern sound**
13. Support a distinctive visual signature around the letter **X**.

The current screening is evidence of the project's present naming state, not a claim of final legal clearance.

#### 3.3.4 Linguistic / Conceptual Basis of DEIXEN

The project's naming rationale associates DEIXEN with the Greek root **deik-**, related to showing, making evident, demonstrating, and evidencing.

The following distinction must be preserved:

> **DEIXEN itself is not presented as an original Greek word. It is a brand construction inspired by the deik- root and its semantic association with showing / making evident / demonstrating.**

This conceptual direction aligns with the product's core progression:

> **Learning → Practice → Scenario → Assessment → Evidence → Growth / Readiness**

The name therefore has a conceptual relationship with the product philosophy rather than depending solely on sound or visual styling.

#### 3.3.5 Visual / Phonetic Rationale

Factors supporting the current name include:

- sharp consonants
- clean vowel structure
- modern sound
- visual distinctiveness
- strong written form
- distinctive X

The current visual rationale is:

> **DEI + X + EN**

The **X** may become a visual signature within the identity system, potentially with a subtle directional character without becoming a literal arrow.

These points are naming rationale, not final Design Execution or a final brand specification.

#### 3.3.6 Naming Status and Verification Boundary

Passing the current filter does not mean that the naming search has produced final legal certainty.

The correct status is:

> **DEIXEN has passed the project's current naming criteria and preliminary screening and is therefore the current working-name candidate.**

However:

> **DEIXEN is NOT yet treated as legally cleared or permanently canonical.**

Future review may still identify:

- a collision not found in preliminary screening
- a trademark issue
- a linguistic issue
- a pronunciation issue
- market ambiguity
- brand-system weakness
- or another substantive reason for reassessment

Therefore:

> **DEIXEN is currently preferred as the working identity, not declared final by this plan.**

The naming search should **not** be reopened as an open-ended exercise merely because the name remains provisional. Reopening should be evidence-driven: a substantive collision, legal concern, linguistic problem, strategic contradiction, or other material issue must justify doing so.

#### 3.3.7 Project-Wide Identity Migration

Identity migration must be performed as part of Corpus preparation.

All current references to AeroBridge should be identified and classified as:

**A. Current Identity References**  
Update to DEIXEN.

**B. Historical / Provenance References**  
Retain AeroBridge where the historical name is part of the record, lineage, or provenance.

**C. External / Legacy References**  
Retain the original name where changing it would distort the source or external reference.

**D. Filenames / Repository Artifacts**  
Evaluate separately according to provenance and reference integrity.

Do not rename a file merely for superficial consistency if doing so would damage provenance, history, or reference integrity.

Guiding rule:

> **Migrate current identity; preserve historical identity where history itself is the subject.**

#### 3.3.8 Identity Consistency Check

At the end of the Cross-Corpus Synchronization Pass, perform an explicit identity consistency check.

Verify:

- no stale AeroBridge references remain in current project identity
- no DEIXEN reference falsely presents the name as a final brand without appropriate authority
- project naming is consistent across artifacts
- product naming is consistent
- learning references are consistent
- design references are consistent
- operating documents are consistent
- handoff material is consistent
- phase/status descriptions are consistent
- there is no unjustified mixture of AeroBridge and DEIXEN

The result should be auditable:

> **AeroBridge remnants → Classified → Migrated / Preserved as History / Preserved as External Reference**

#### 3.3.9 Identity Impact Check

The project must not assume that a rename is purely cosmetic.

After identity migration, verify that it has not unintentionally introduced:

- product-scope changes
- architecture changes
- learning changes
- curriculum changes
- domain changes
- decision changes
- ownership changes
- semantic drift

Guiding rule:

> **Changing the project name must not silently change the project itself.**

Any artifact that uses the naming migration as an opportunity to introduce substantive change must be reviewed as a separate substantive change.

## 5. Fundamental Preparation Order

The project must not be reviewed as an unstructured pile of files.

The intended dependency-aware order is:

> **Amadeus Evidence → Operating Layer → Corpus Authority Map → Identity Migration & Naming Evaluation → Amadeus Reference Stabilization → Decision / Current-State Reconciliation → Product / Architecture Foundation → Curriculum / Coach Foundation → Learning Foundation → Design Foundation → Cross-Corpus Synchronization → Evidence / State Confirmation → Full Pre-Opus Adversarial Audit → Carry-Forward Registers → Opus Readiness → Final Pre-Opus Checkpoint → Handoff**

Higher-level foundations should be sufficiently stabilized before dependent artifacts are strengthened against them.

This is an ordered working sequence, not a claim that later review can never expose issues in earlier layers.

**Upstream rechecking:** when a later review materially invalidates a conclusion reached earlier, do not restart the earlier phase in full by default. Identify the affected scope, recheck that scope against the new evidence, update the affected artifact(s), and resume forward once the affected path is stable. Record the change and its disposition in the Carry-Forward Register, whether resolved immediately or carried forward.

## 7. Phase 1 — Amadeus Intake and Evidence Stabilization

This is the first substantive project block.

The objective is not to create a polished Amadeus document.

The objective is to establish the strongest evidence layer that can responsibly be built around Amadeus.

### Command-Level Truth

For important commands, examine:

- syntax
- accepted structure
- prerequisites
- state dependencies
- output behavior
- constraints
- error behavior
- relationships to other commands
- training relevance
- confidence / evidence

### Workflow Truth

Do not stop at:

> “Command A works.”  
> “Command B works.”

Ask instead:

- Does A actually establish the conditions required for B?
- What are the preconditions?
- What is the state transition?
- What changes in the PNR / pricing state?
- Is the workflow itself valid?
- Does the educational example reflect real operation, or only parser behavior?

### Implementation Truth vs Domain Truth

This distinction must remain explicit:

**Implementation Truth**  
= what the current DEIXEN implementation demonstrably does.

**Domain Truth**  
= what Amadeus / the relevant operational domain actually does in the real-world environment.

Implementation Truth can be verified through implementation evidence.

Domain Truth must not be inferred merely because a simulator, parser, or prototype supports a behavior.

Where evidence is insufficient:

> **VERIFY / EXTERNAL VALIDATION / UNKNOWN**

Do not fill gaps with intuition or cross-GDS assumptions.

## 18. Phase 12 — Full Corpus Adversarial Pre-Opus Audit

Sonnet, with ChatGPT acting as an independent reviewer, performs a final deliberate attempt to break the prepared foundation before Opus.

The question is not:

> “Does everything look good?”

The question is:

> **“How could this Corpus still be wrong even though it appears coherent?”**

Look for:

### Authority Failures
An artifact claims or decides something outside its legitimate authority.

### Contradictions
Two artifacts make materially incompatible claims.

### Hidden Assumptions
Important information is treated as established without sufficient evidence.

### Scope Drift
A decision is being used outside its intended scope.

### Stale References
Outdated filenames, phase descriptions, or cross-references remain active.

### Dependency Gaps
A changed decision or fact has not propagated to affected artifacts.

### Domain Overclaims
Especially in Amadeus.

### Learning Overclaims
Learning principles or hypotheses are presented as proven DEIXEN-specific facts.

### Implementation Leakage
Prototype or implementation behavior is being mistaken for requirement, specification, or domain truth.

### False Closure
An open matter is written in a definitive way without sufficient authority.

### Missing Evidence
Material claims lack enough support.

### Unnecessary Complexity
Material is added because it might be useful rather than because it is justified.

## 26. Definition of Done

The Pre-Opus Foundation is ready for handoff when we can demonstrate:

### Knowledge

The Amadeus evidence / reference layer has been organized and reviewed to the fullest extent supported by available evidence.

### Governance

Authority boundaries between truth, recommendation, decision, history, and uncertainty are explicit.

### Identity

The Corpus uses **DEIXEN** consistently as the current working identity where appropriate, while preserving **AeroBridge** only where required for historical, provenance, or external-reference integrity.

DEIXEN remains explicitly marked as **provisional / under test**.

The naming rationale, criteria, and preliminary-screening boundary are documented and traceable.

### Corpus

Every artifact has a known purpose, status, authority relationship, and dependency context.

### Synchronization

Material changes have been traced through all legitimately affected artifacts.

### Product

The Product / Architecture foundation is consistent with current decisions and the available domain evidence.

### Learning

Curriculum + Coach + LDS + LXA are sufficiently aligned and do not contradict the product / domain foundation.

### Design

The Design Foundation is coherent without prematurely entering Design Execution.

### Evidence / State

Evidence and state assumptions are explicit, bounded, and not overstated.

### Unknowns

Every material unresolved item is recorded rather than hidden.

### Validation

Every remaining external / SME validation requirement is known and explicitly tracked.

### Adversarial Preparation

Sonnet + ChatGPT have made a deliberate attempt to identify why the Corpus could still fail.

### Handoff

Opus can enter the project, understand its structure and context, see what is established versus uncertain, and independently challenge the work without reconstructing the project's history from scratch.

### Pre-Opus Readiness Gate

This section yields exactly one of two outcomes.

**PASS** — every condition above is satisfied; remaining unresolved material matters are explicitly identified, classified, owned, and carried forward rather than silently dropped; every required external / SME validation need is tracked; and no material issue remains untracked. Opus can understand and independently challenge the Corpus without reconstructing project history from conversational memory.

**NOT PASS** — at least one condition above is unmet, a material issue exists and is not tracked, or the Corpus is not genuinely challenge-ready. A NOT PASS determination must name the specific condition, register, or artifact where the gap was found — a bare declaration of "not ready" does not satisfy this gate.

A PASS does not require every OPEN question, validation requirement, or Opus Candidate item to be resolved — only that such items are visible, bounded, and traceable. Tracked uncertainty is compatible with PASS; hidden or untracked uncertainty is not.

## 28. Strategic Principle

> **“DEIXEN is as verified, strengthened, synchronized, explicit, and challenge-ready as we can responsibly make it before an independent Opus review.”**
