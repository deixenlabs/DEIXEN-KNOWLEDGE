1. PROJECT POSITION

AeroBridge is a professional training platform intended to develop practical, work-oriented competence in Amadeus/GDS air reservations and ticketing.

The product is not merely:

- a generic LMS,
- a course library,
- a command memorization tool,
- a simulator with lessons attached,
- or a visual dashboard.

Its purpose is to connect:

knowledge  learning  practice  realistic performance  evidence  progression  readiness

The intended learning progression includes:

Learn
 Retrieve
 Practice
 Feedback
 Correction
 Repeat
 Independent Performance
 Assessment
 Evidence
 Progression
 Reinforcement
 Transfer
 Readiness

The Learning Experience Architecture is the layer that translates the approved learning system into a coherent product experience.

It sits upstream of the later visual identity/design work and downstream of the current Product, Learning, Knowledge, and Decision foundations.

13. REQUIRED EXPERIENCE COVERAGE

Evaluate and architect at least the following.

A. Entry / Orientation

What the learner needs to know:

- where they are,
- what they are doing,
- why it matters,
- what success means,
- and what happens next.

B. Learning / Curriculum

How instructional material prepares the learner for action.

Avoid unnecessary passive-content architecture.

C. Retrieval

When prior knowledge is intentionally activated and how that experience relates to performance.

D. Guided Practice

How support helps the learner move toward independent performance.

E. Independent Practice

How support is reduced when the Learning Design requires independence.

F. Reminal / Operational Practice

How the terminal experience functions as a meaningful operational environment.

G. Scenarios

How scenarios provide operational context and connect skills into meaningful workflows.

H. Assessment

How assessment is distinct from ordinary practice where required.

I. Feedback / Correction

How system feedback, Coach guidance, correction, and retry interact.

J. Evidence

How legitimate observations become usable learner evidence.

K. Progression

How evidence affects learner progression without false claims.

L. Reinforcement / Retention

How previously learned capability returns into later experience.

M. Transfer

How skills are deliberately encountered in changed or broader contexts.

N. Failure / Recovery

How the learner recovers from:

- errors,
- repeated failure,
- confusion,
- help requests,
- interruption,
- abandonment,
- and re-entry.

O. Continuity / Resume

What survives across navigation, retries, interruptions, and future sessions.

System Behavior
 what the system does/observes

Evidence

Exit
 how the learner leaves

Failure / Recovery
 what happens if the intended outcome fails

Dependencies
 what external system/artifact supports it
