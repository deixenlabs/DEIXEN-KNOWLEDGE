## How to use it

- Read the synthesis as a proposal for the Knowledge foundation, not as a new authority.
- Use the CSV to track missing evidence, owner domains, status, dependencies, and review gates.
- Preserve the original Claude report verbatim; compare Claude's forthcoming separate comments against both the source and this synthesis.
- Reconcile all material proposals with the current strategy, Constitution, decision record, resolution register, Verified Reference, standards framework, app reality, and owner approval process.
- Keep direct Amadeus facts, adjacent learning research, practitioner judgement, learner evidence, implementation facts, market evidence, and commercial hypotheses in separate evidence lanes.
- Do not promote proposals into canonical requirements by moving files or changing status labels.

## Important limitations

- The research review is directed rather than systematic; many study findings in the source report were read from abstracts, not full texts. The original report's limitations must travel with the claims.
- O*NET is US occupational context, not proof of Saudi, Oman, or UAE job requirements.
- IATA competency guidance is useful by analogy but is written for pilots/instructors/evaluators, not reservation agents.
- Learning-science and medical-simulation findings are adjacent evidence, not proof of DEIXEN effectiveness.
- Competitor, credential, course, and pricing snapshots dated 5 November 2026 must be refreshed before public launch or commercial commitments.
- The synthesis has not incorporated Claude's missing separate comments/report, nor the pending practitioner response to CP-001.
- No GitHub or local project source files were modified by preparing this package. The files here are separate working artifacts only.
