Identity rules · provisional

What the identity is, and what it must never become

It is

A working document.

Rules, fields and a margin. One sheet of record, the Terminal, which starts right under the header; everything else sits on the desk.

Two inks.

Black for the record and for what you type. Blue for DEIXEN, and only DEIXEN.

Level with the line.

DEIXEN speaks in the margin beside the line it means, joined by a leader. At 768 the margin docks under the Terminal; on phones it folds into a drawer that opens on demand.

Three voices.

Record in mono, interface in sans, DEIXEN in a reading face (Naskh in Arabic). A kind of text never borrows another's face.

Words on controls.

A button says what it does. The only arrow is the proof mark, because there a line really moves.

Numbered like a manual.

Two-digit figures in a number column for DEIXEN's own order; Amadeus numbers stay where Amadeus puts them.

Status in words.

Correct in DEIXEN, Next, Optional, Completed, or a dash. One qualitative status in Growth, tied to its basis.

Calm motion.

Motion only to explain a change: the proof mark draws once. With reduced motion, everything is simply there.

Bilingual by construction.

The margin changes sides with the language; the record never does. Arabic sizes are set one step larger for equal colour.

It must never become

Cards in rows, or a hero.

No soft shadows, rounded containers, stat cards, progress rings or bars, badges or pills; no display headline over a single button.

Decoration.

No gradients at all, no glass, no orbs, no photographs of planes, globes, boarding passes or radar.

Blue on the record.

Nothing drawn over or behind an Amadeus character: no tint, no highlight, no fade, no underline in the text columns.

A serif headline.

The reading face is DEIXEN's voice; it never becomes display type.

Eyebrow capitals.

No letter-spaced uppercase labels above headings.

A hacker terminal.

No green on black, no scanlines, no blinking effects, no typing sounds.

Scores.

No numbers that grade the learner: no percentages, points, streaks or readiness scores.

The wordmark set in a font.

Never retyped, recoloured, mirrored, outlined or stretched; never smaller than 14 px cap height. Clear space: one cell on every side.

Invented Amadeus.

No screen, code or message that is not a verified rendering. Where nothing is verified: a labelled placeholder.
